PodBridge · Files workflow

Sync a supported iPod without a desktop iTunes workflow.

PodBridge moves music you own from Files on iPhone or iPad to a supported click-wheel iPod. It is not a music service and does not remove DRM.

Get PodBridge free

Before you start

  • Keep a recoverable backup.
  • Confirm the iPod appears in Files through your cable or adapter.
  • Use the tested Classic 7G profile where possible.
  • Start with one track and verify the library.

Stock firmware and Rockbox

PodBridge does not install or configure Rockbox. Use the destination appropriate for the firmware already on your iPod and follow the compatibility guidance in the app.

Authorized files only

Use only music you are authorized to use. PodBridge does not download Apple Music or iTunes Store DRM files.

PodBridge is not affiliated with Apple.
