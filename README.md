# PodBridge

PodBridge transfers music you own from Files on your iPhone or iPad to a
supported click-wheel iPod. It works offline, preserves playlists and artwork,
and creates a verified backup before changing the iPod library.

## Download

[PodBridge on the App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/podbridge/id6810086957)

## Support

Please use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/dbalantaev/PodBridge-Store/issues)
for setup questions, bug reports, and device-compatibility feedback. Include
the app version, iOS/iPadOS version, iPod model, and a short description of
what happened. Do not attach music files, private paths, or credentials.

## Compatibility

PodBridge requires iOS/iPadOS 17 or later and a supported click-wheel iPod that
appears in Files. iPod Classic 160 GB (late 2009 / 7th generation) is the only
profile tested on real hardware for the current release; other profiles are
experimental. Back up the iPod and begin with one track.

PodBridge works only with music you are authorized to use. It does not
distribute music and is not affiliated with Apple.
