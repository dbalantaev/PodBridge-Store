PODBRIDGE · PRESS

The missing onboarding step in the iPod revival.

PodBridge helps owners move music they already own from Files on iPhone or iPad to a supported click-wheel iPod, with backup-first guidance and explicit compatibility limits.

View PodBridge on the App Store

Fact sheet

  • Tested baseline: iPod Classic 160 GB, late 2009 / 7th generation.
  • Other profiles, including nano and mini, are experimental.
  • Workflow: Files → supported click-wheel iPod.
  • Safety: back up first and start with one track.
  • Rights: authorized music only; no DRM bypass.
  • PodBridge is not affiliated with Apple.

Demo angle

Show the physical iPod, the cable or adapter, Files, one-track transfer, and the resulting library. Do not claim Apple Music or iTunes DRM extraction.

Contact

For setup questions or a demo request, use the public GitHub Issues route. Do not attach music files, private paths, or credentials.
